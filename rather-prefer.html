Would rather и would prefer

I'd rather + глагол без to: I'd rather stay at home.

I'd rather + кто-то + Past Simple: I'd rather you didn't tell anyone.

I'd prefer + to-инфинитив: I'd prefer to stay at home.

Прошлое: I'd rather have stayed / I'd rather they hadn't stayed.

Упражнение B

Complete the second sentence so that it means the same as the first, using the word given.

Используй слово, выделенное жирным (rather / prefer / preferred)

rather I criticizing me.

2.I wish they hadn't stayed so long.

preferred I so long.

3.I don't want to think about that problem now.

rather I about that problem now.

rather I me so late at night.

prefer I me the money now.

rather I at home.
